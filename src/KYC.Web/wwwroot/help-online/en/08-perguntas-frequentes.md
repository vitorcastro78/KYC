# Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

## General

**Do I need to install anything on my PC?**  
No. The application runs in the browser; you only need network access to your institution’s server.

**Can I have several cases open for the same NIF?**  
The platform can show history by NIF in the list; check the internal policy on reuse versus a new case.

**What is the 0–100 score?**  
An automatic risk summary based on signals. It does not replace human decision-making (GDPR Art. 22).

## Screening

**How long does screening take?**  
Typically 2–10 minutes, depending on the number of parties and server load. Complex cases with many UBOs may take longer.

**Can I close the browser during screening?**  
Yes. Screening runs on the server. Reopen the case details to see the result.

**Does rerunning screening delete my signal confirmations?**  
Re-screening regenerates automatic signals; previous manual confirmations may be affected depending on the engine rules — always review after re-screening.

## Compliance

**Do all UBOs have to be verified?**  
Yes, for approval, when there are parties with a UBO or corporate-body role in the case.

**When should I report a SAR?**  
When you identify a suspicious transaction under Law 83/2017. The yellow banner suggests it; the decision belongs to the analyst/compliance function.

**What is EDD?**  
Enhanced due diligence — requires source of funds and a second approver.

## Reports

**Is the report suitable for regulatory records?**  
It is an operational KYC report with automated rationale. Check with compliance whether it meets all internal requirements.

**Can I edit the report in the system?**  
The report is generated automatically; use case notes, manual signals and the audit trail for additions.

## Roles

**I am an analyst but cannot see Administration**  
This is normal — the Admin menu is only for `KYC.Admin` (and auditing for `KYC.Auditor`).

**How do I request access?**  
Contact your institution’s application administrator (Entra ID role).
